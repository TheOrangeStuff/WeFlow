# Changelog

## 1.8.0 — 2026-11-13

- Picking a SmugMug gallery is simpler: press Choose Gallery… and pick from the list, or
  search it. You can still paste a gallery link there.
- Your galleries load as soon as you're signed in, so the list is ready when you open it.
- To make a new gallery, choose Create a gallery… at the top of the list. You name it on
  its own step, so it's always clear where the photos will go.
- The Keywords box is gone from the event editor.
- Pinch (or Ctrl + scroll) to zoom into the before/after preview. Drag or scroll to look
  around, and double-click to see the whole photo again. The split line stays on the photo.
- The main screen is one panel now: a bigger card icon, "Insert a card", and below it
  where photos go, with Switch right beside it.
- Your events, settings and sign-ins carry over unchanged.

## 1.7.2 — 2026-11-13

- Your SmugMug galleries and Flickr albums are easy to find again: click the empty Gallery
  box, or the new arrow, to open the list. You can also type to search it.
- Every dropdown now shows an arrow, so it is clear you can open it.
- Your events, settings and sign-ins carry over unchanged.

## 1.7.1 — 2026-11-13

- In the event editor, the watermark margin now sits with the other watermark settings.
- The "More" section is gone, and thin lines now separate the editor's sections.
- Your events, settings and sign-ins carry over unchanged.

## 1.7.0 — 2026-11-13

- While copying from a card, WeFlow shows the speed in MB/s, how many photos are left and
  about how long it will take.
- Uploads to SmugMug or Flickr show the same: speed, photos left and time left.
- When the copy is done, WeFlow says so and how long it took, even after you take the
  card out or when importing a folder.
- The "Resize long edge" option is gone. Photos are always kept at full size.
- Your events, settings and sign-ins carry over unchanged.

## 1.6.8 — 2026-11-13

- While a button takes a few seconds, the "Flowing…" cover now shows the We Run
  Huntsville logo looping instead of a thin bar.
- Your events, settings and sign-ins carry over unchanged.

## 1.6.7 — 2026-11-13

- Fixed: on accounts with more than 2000 galleries, the newest galleries were missing

- Your events, settings and sign-ins carry over unchanged.

## 1.6.6 — 2026-11-13

- When a button takes a few seconds, such as **Connect**, the window greys out and shows
  "Flowing…" until it is done.
- Your events, settings and sign-ins carry over unchanged.

## 1.6.5 — 2026-11-13

- Fixed: the gallery list could stay on "Loading" for minutes on accounts with many
  galleries. WeFlow now fetches it much faster.
- The gallery list is loaded once per session. Press the reload button next to it to
  see galleries you made on the SmugMug website since.
- Your events, settings and sign-ins carry over unchanged.

## 1.6.4 — 2026-11-13

- Fixed: pressing **Connect** could say the keychain was not available. If your Mac
  refuses WeFlow access to an older keychain item, WeFlow now saves to a new one instead.
- If you press **Deny** on a keychain prompt, WeFlow carries on. You may need to enter
  your API key again.
- Your events, settings and sign-ins carry over unchanged.

## 1.6.3 — 2026-11-13

- On Mac, WeFlow no longer keeps asking to use your keychain. The first time you open
  this version it may ask a few more times, once per saved item. Enter your password
  and press **Always Allow**.
- After that, WeFlow asks at most once when you install a new version.
- Your events, settings and sign-ins carry over unchanged.

## 1.6.2 — 2026-11-13

- New **Check for updates** button at the bottom of Settings. It tells you if you're up
  to date, or offers the new version to download.
- Your events, settings and sign-ins carry over unchanged.

## 1.6.1 — 2026-11-13

- The gallery list shows your 20 newest galleries. Pick **More galleries…** at the bottom
  to see 20 more. Typing a name still finds any gallery.
- The gallery list loads faster and waits longer for a slow connection.
- New **Copy log** button in Settings. If something goes wrong, press it and paste the
  text into your message.
- Your events, settings and sign-ins carry over unchanged.

## 1.6.0 — 2026-11-13

- Make a new gallery without leaving WeFlow. Press **+** next to the gallery box, type
  a name and press **Create**. The new gallery is picked for the event right away.
- New SmugMug galleries are Unlisted: people with the link can see them. Flickr makes a
  new album when its first photo goes up.
- When you set up WeFlow for the first time, you can now pick your gallery from a list
  as soon as you have signed in.
- Hover over a button or switch to see what it does.
- Your events, settings and sign-ins carry over unchanged.

## 1.5.0 — 2026-11-13

- Your originals are now the copy you keep. Once a photo is uploaded and in its album,
  WeFlow deletes the edited copy, so you no longer have two sets of every photo.
- Want the edited copies too? Turn on **Keep processed copies after upload** in the
  event. They then go into the event's processed folder, as before.
- Photos waiting to upload are kept out of sight until they are up.
- Edited copies you already have stay where they are. Your events, settings and
  sign-ins carry over unchanged.

## 1.4.0 — 2026-11-11

- Pick your gallery from a list. In an event, the SmugMug gallery (or Flickr album) box
  now shows the galleries on your account, newest first. Type to search.
- Pasting a gallery link or typing a Flickr album title still works.
- Press the reload button next to the box to see galleries you just made.
- Your events, settings and sign-ins carry over unchanged.

## 1.3.0 — 2026-11-11

- New **Auto / Paced** switch at the top of the window. Auto works as before: insert a
  card and your photos go all the way to SmugMug or Flickr.
- Paced asks before each big step. After copying, press **Process** to auto-tune the
  photos. Once they are tuned, press **Upload** to send them.
- Switching back to Auto sends any waiting photos on their way.
- Held photos now show an **Upload** button, which replaces **Release**.
- Your events, settings and sign-ins carry over unchanged.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-11

- Name your finished photos the Lightroom way: custom name with a sequence number, date
  and filename, the camera's filename, and more. Find it under **Export** when you edit
  an event. Originals keep their names.
- Quality now goes from 0 to 100, like Lightroom.
- New **Limit file size**: WeFlow picks the best quality that keeps each photo under the
  size you set.
- Save your export settings as a **preset** and pick it in any event.
- Photos already processed keep their names. Your events, settings and sign-ins carry
  over unchanged.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-11

- WeFlow tells you when a newer version is out. A bar in the main window offers
  **Download**, which opens the download page, or **Skip** to hide that version.
  It only appears while WeFlow is idle, and can be switched off in Settings.
- After an update, WeFlow shows what's new once.
- Connecting SmugMug or Flickr asks for your own API key straight away, with a link to
  the page where you get one. Keys you already entered keep working.
- Installers are now also published to the public
  [weflow-releases](https://github.com/TheOrangeStuff/weflow-releases) page.
- WeFlow for Mac now needs a Mac with Apple silicon. Intel Macs are no longer supported.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-11

Built from `SPEC.md`, milestone by milestone. Nothing here has yet been tried with a
real card, a real SmugMug or Flickr account, or an installed build; see
`docs/manual-test.md`.

### M7 — Polish
- 200 ms crossfade between status-panel states; sliding activity drawer; animated toggles.
- Empty states: "Earlier cards" and the Activity handle appear only when there is
  something to show; the preview and the activity log explain themselves when empty.
- Every user-facing error is checked to be plain language, two short sentences at most.
- End-user `README.md` and the hardware checklist `docs/manual-test.md`.

### M6 — Packaging
- PyInstaller one-folder spec; `.app` + `.dmg` script for macOS; Inno Setup installer for Windows.
- Signing and notarisation hooks that read credentials from environment variables.
- App icon (`packaging/make_icons.py`), `--smoke-test` launch check, CI build jobs with artifacts.
- Pushing a `v<version>` tag publishes the installers to a GitHub Release.

### M5 — Event editor, Settings, first run
- Event editor (640 px): Destination, Look, Watermark, collapsed "More", inline validation.
- Live before/after preview with a draggable split, debounced and rendered off the UI thread (FR-04).
- Settings: library folder, SmugMug / Flickr connect, reconnect and disconnect, own API key,
  card sound, start at login.
- First-run window: library → connect a service (skippable) → first event.

### M4 — Main window
- The working face wired to the pipeline through `ui/bridge.py`.
- Card notice "Card X → Event" with Cancel and a 5 s auto-start (FR-11); event switcher when
  no event is active.
- Eject with success / failure reported in the window (FR-16).
- Tray icon with four states, menu, throttled notifications, hide-to-tray while working.

### M3 — Theme and widgets
- Design tokens → generated QSS, light and dark, following the OS live.
- EventCard, StatusPanel (all nine states), StepTracker, CardRow, Chip, ActivityDrawer.
- WCAG AA contrast test; screenshots of every state in both themes under `tests/artifacts/`.

### M1 + M2 — Core
- No v0.1 prototype was available, so the engine was written from the spec and hardened
  in the same step (see `DECISIONS.md`).
- Events with stable ids and frozen folders; settings and events saved atomically.
- Secrets in the OS keychain only, with migration of any plain-text v0.1 values.
- Card detection (macOS, Windows, fake volumes for tests) and eject.
- Verified copy: SHA-256 while copying, fsync, re-hash; rename by capture time; de-duplication.
- Auto white balance, auto tone, watermark, EXIF orientation, resize; atomic writes.
- SmugMug and Flickr uploaders; upload and add-to-album as separate, resumable steps.
- Pipeline with saved state after every transition, retries with back-off, hold / release,
  retry failed, resume on launch, coalesced typed events, `stop()`.
- Tooling: `pyproject.toml` with pinned dependencies, ruff, `mypy --strict` on core, pytest,
  GitHub Actions on Ubuntu, macOS and Windows.
